# Contact

FindStarlink is a fan-made project, and it is **NOT affiliated with SpaceX or Starlink in any way**.

If your question isn't answered in the [FAQ](faq.md), or you've found a problem, you can email [dev@findstarlink.com](mailto:dev@findstarlink.com) and I'll try to help. I'd also love to hear from you if you saw (or didn't see) Starlink using the timings.

## Cancelling reminders

Set a reminder you no longer need? Open the results entry it belongs to and tap **Cancel** - the button that replaced **Remind me**. See [Set reminders](set-reminders.md#cancelling-a-reminder).

## Privacy policy

The privacy policy for the Find Starlink mobile apps is available [here](https://findstarlink.com/privacy-app.html).

Thanks for using this, and enjoy gazing at the beautiful night sky! :)
