# Welcome

FindStarlink shows you exactly when the Starlink satellites will pass over **your** location - pick a place, and you get a list of timings with which direction to look and how bright the pass will be. It tracks the leader of every visible "chain" of satellites, so when you spot one, the rest of the train follows right behind it.

## See it tonight, in 3 steps

1. **Choose your location** - by name or coordinates. See [Finding your location](finding-your-location.md).
2. **Check the timings** - the results page lists every sighting over the next 5 days, sorted into good, average and poor visibility. See [Reading the results](reading-the-results.md).
3. **Look up at the right time** - and if you're on mobile, set a reminder so you don't miss it. See [Set reminders](set-reminders.md).
