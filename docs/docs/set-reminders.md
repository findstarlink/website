# Set reminders

!!! note "Mobile only"

    Reminders are available in the **Android** and **iOS** apps. The website can't send notifications, but the apps have you covered.

## Remind me

Every entry on the results page has a **Remind me** button. Tap it, and the app schedules a notification so you don't have to keep watching the clock.

Depending on how far away the sighting is, you'll be notified:

- **30 minutes** before the sighting,
- or **10 minutes** before if there's less than half an hour left,
- or right at pass time if the sighting is imminent.

The app confirms with a message like *"Okay, you will be reminded 30 mins before this timing"*.

## Notification permission

=== "Android"

    On Android 13 and newer, the app asks for permission to send notifications the first time you tap **Remind me**. If you don't allow it, the reminder can't be shown - you can enable notifications later in the system Settings, under Apps → Find Starlink → Notifications.

=== "iOS"

    The app asks for permission to send notifications the first time you tap **Remind me**. If permission was denied, you'll see *"Sorry, did not get the permission to display notifications!"* - you can enable notifications later in the system Settings, under Find Starlink → Notifications.

## Cancelling a reminder

Changed your plans (or the clouds rolled in)? The **Remind me** button on that entry turns into a **Cancel** button once a reminder is set. Tap it and the reminder is removed - you'll see *"Okay, the alarm has been canceled"*.
