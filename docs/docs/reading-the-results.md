# Reading the results

The results page lists every expected Starlink sighting at your location over the **next 5 days**.

## The header

At the top you'll see the place you searched for, along with that day's **Sunrise** and **Sunset** times, and the time zone the timings are shown in:

> All times displayed in your local time zone - **all times are local, you do NOT need to convert them**. Daylight-savings/summer/winter are already included, if required.

## Good, average and poor visibility

The sightings are grouped into three lists:

- **Timings with good visibility** - the best ones to watch.
- **Timings with average visibility** - worth a look if there's nothing good.
- **Timings with poor visibility** - faint; you might not see anything.

## Each entry

Every entry shows:

- the **date and time** of the sighting,
- the **satellite name** (for example *STARLINK-1234*),
- **BRIGHT** or **DIM** with a number in brackets - this is the brightness *magnitude*, and the **smaller the number, the brighter it is**,
- how long the pass lasts, e.g. *"for 5 mins"*.

Tap or click **details** on an entry to see which way to look: a compass direction like *"Look from **NW** to **SE**"*, plus the elevation of the pass from the horizon (start, max, end). The exact degrees are shown on mobile.

## A ±10 minute caveat

Orbit predictions aren't perfect - **actual times may vary by 10 mins**. Please [let me know](mailto:dev@findstarlink.com) if you see Starlink using the app (or if you don't).

## When nothing good is visible

If the newest Starlink isn't very visible at your location during the next 5 days, you'll see:

> Sorry, the newest Starlink isn't very visible at your location during the next 5 days. This might change in the coming weeks, due to changing orbits. The older Starlinks might still be visible (please see below).

Check the average and poor visibility lists - some older chains may still be visible.

## Share your results

=== "Web"

    Use the **Share** button to copy a **Link** to your results, or share them on **Twitter** or **Facebook**. The link opens the same timings for the same place.

=== "Android"

    Tap **Share** to share a link to the same results using your device's native share menu. Your last results are also cached for **24 hours**, so you can still see them offline.
